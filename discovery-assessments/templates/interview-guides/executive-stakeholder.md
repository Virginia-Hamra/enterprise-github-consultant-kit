# Executive Stakeholder Interview Guide

> 30 minutes. Goal: align on **business outcomes, constraints, and definition of success.**

## Objectives
- Understand strategic drivers
- Confirm executive priorities & non-negotiables
- Surface political / organizational constraints
- Validate success metrics

## Opening (5 min)
- Introductions, agenda, time box
- Confirm recording / note-taking permission

## Core questions (20 min)
1. What business outcome triggered this engagement?
2. What does "success" look like in 6 months? 18 months?
3. What are the top risks you're worried about?
4. Are there any board-level or audit commitments tied to this work?
5. Who needs to see results, and in what form?
6. What's the single most important thing we should *not* break?
7. Are there organizational changes coming that we should plan around?
8. How do you want to be kept informed (cadence, format)?

## Close (5 min)
- Recap key points
- Confirm next checkpoints
- Ask: "Who else should we talk to?"
