# Developer Team Interview Guide

> 45 minutes. Pair with `../questionnaires/developer-experience.md`. Aim for a small group (3–6 ICs + 1–2 leads).

## Objectives
- Hear unfiltered developer experience
- Validate platform team's view of pain points
- Identify quick wins

## Opening (5 min)
- Frame: "We're not auditing you — we want your honest experience."

## Day in the life (15 min)
- Walk us through a typical PR from start to merge
- Where do you wait? Where do you context-switch?
- What tools do you use beyond GitHub?

## Friction (10 min)
- Top 3 frustrations
- Things you've worked around vs. raised
- Onboarding experience

## Golden paths (10 min)
- Templates / scaffolders you actually use
- Where you build from scratch and why

## AI tooling (5 min, if in scope)
- Current usage, perceived value
- Concerns / hesitations

## Close (5 min)
- "If we could fix one thing in 90 days..."
