# Platform Team Interview Guide

> 60 minutes. Pair with `../questionnaires/current-state-platform.md`.

## Objectives
- Map current GitHub footprint and ownership
- Understand operational pain points
- Identify automation gaps & opportunities

## Opening (5 min)
- Roles around the table
- Goals of the session

## Current state (20 min)
- Walk the questionnaire responses; deepen on TBDs
- Live tour: enterprise / org settings, sample repo, runners

## Operations (15 min)
- Change management for org-level settings
- On-call rotations, runbooks
- Most common support tickets
- Top-3 toil items

## Automation & IaC (10 min)
- Terraform / API / scripts already in use
- Source of truth for config
- Drift detection

## Wishlist (5 min)
- "If you had one quarter of dedicated platform investment..."

## Close (5 min)
- Confirm artifacts to be shared
- Schedule follow-ups
