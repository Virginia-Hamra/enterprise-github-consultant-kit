# Security Team Interview Guide

> 60 minutes. Pair with `../questionnaires/security-and-compliance.md`.

## Objectives
- Map compliance scope & current control posture
- Understand GHAS adoption & blockers
- Align on incident response model

## Opening (5 min)
## Compliance landscape (10 min)
- Frameworks, audit cycle, evidence today
- Top audit findings on dev tooling

## GHAS posture (20 min)
- Coverage today vs. licensed
- Triage SLAs and ownership
- Custom patterns & exclusions
- Pain points (false positives, scaling)

## Branch & repo controls (10 min)
- Rulesets / classic protection
- Bypass list governance
- CODEOWNERS adoption

## Audit & IR (10 min)
- Audit log streaming destination, retention
- Critical alert rules
- IR playbooks & last tabletop exercise
