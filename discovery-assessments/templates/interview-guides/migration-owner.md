# Migration Owner Interview Guide

> 60 minutes. Pair with `../questionnaires/migration-readiness.md`.

## Objectives
- Confirm scope, scale, and constraints
- Identify wave strategy & risks
- Align on cutover and validation approach

## Opening (5 min)

## Inventory & scope (15 min)
- Walk source inventory
- Archive vs. migrate decisions
- Out-of-scope repos & justifications

## Constraints (15 min)
- Compliance / chain-of-custody requirements
- Downtime tolerance
- Blackout windows
- Network / security constraints

## Wave strategy (15 min)
- Proposed wave breakdown
- Validation criteria per wave
- Rollback criteria

## Communications (5 min)
- Status reporting
- Cutover comms model
- Stakeholder registry
