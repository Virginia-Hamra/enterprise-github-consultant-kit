# Pre-Engagement Checklist

Complete before kickoff. Owner: Delivery lead.

## Scope & contracts
- [ ] SOW / MSA signed and stored
- [ ] Scope, deliverables, success criteria documented
- [ ] Out-of-scope items explicitly listed
- [ ] Engagement duration & key milestones agreed
- [ ] Change-control process agreed

## Stakeholders
- [ ] Executive sponsor identified
- [ ] Customer technical lead identified
- [ ] RACI drafted
- [ ] Communication plan (cadence, channels) agreed

## Access & logistics
- [ ] Customer-side accounts requested (GitHub, IdP, cloud, ticketing)
- [ ] VPN / network access requirements understood
- [ ] Data handling / NDA confirmed
- [ ] Tooling allowlist (laptop, VPN, customer collab tools)

## Internal prep
- [ ] Delivery team assigned
- [ ] Engagement folder created (`discovery-assessments/engagements/<slug>/`)
- [ ] Initial risk register seeded
- [ ] Kickoff agenda prepared
