# Engagement Handoff Checklist

Owner: All roles. Complete before final invoice.

## Deliverables
- [ ] All in-scope deliverables accepted by customer (signoff captured)
- [ ] Final architecture diagrams delivered
- [ ] Runbooks delivered & walked through
- [ ] Compliance evidence package delivered
- [ ] Decision log and ADRs delivered

## Knowledge transfer
- [ ] Hands-on training sessions completed
- [ ] Recording & materials shared
- [ ] Q&A / office hours held
- [ ] Customer team can perform top-10 ops tasks unassisted

## Operational handoff
- [ ] Support escalation path documented
- [ ] On-call / incident response procedures handed over
- [ ] Monitoring / alerting ownership transferred
- [ ] Open risks & follow-up items logged with owners

## Engagement closeout
- [ ] Customer satisfaction survey completed
- [ ] Retrospective held (internal)
- [ ] Lessons learned captured in toolkit
- [ ] Customer access to consultant accounts revoked
- [ ] Customer data sanitized from local machines
- [ ] Engagement folder archived securely
