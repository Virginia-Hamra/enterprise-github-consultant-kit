# How to Ask Better Questions

Asking is a craft. Most consulting failures are not bad answers — they are **good answers to the wrong questions**. This guide is for building the muscle to ask questions that actually move the engagement forward.

Read in order:

1. [`principles.md`](principles.md) — what questions are *for*, and the mindset behind asking
2. [`question-types.md`](question-types.md) — the toolkit: open, closed, funnel, layering, hypothesis-driven
3. [`probing-and-followup.md`](probing-and-followup.md) — going deeper when answers are shallow (5 whys, laddering, silence)
4. [`audience-tuning.md`](audience-tuning.md) — adjusting for executives, security, ICs, ops, legal
5. [`difficult-conversations.md`](difficult-conversations.md) — challenging assumptions, surfacing conflict, handling pushback
6. [`question-bank.md`](question-bank.md) — reusable question library by domain (identity, security, CI/CD, migration, Copilot, ...)
7. [`anti-patterns.md`](anti-patterns.md) — leading questions, jargon traps, multi-stacks, false binaries

> **TL;DR**
> - Ask **open** questions to discover; ask **closed** questions to confirm.
> - One question at a time. Stop talking after the question mark.
> - Listen for what's *not* said as much as what is.
> - Repeat answers back in your own words to confirm understanding.
> - When you don't understand, **say so**. Confused consultants ask better questions than confident ones.
